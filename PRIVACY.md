# Privacy Policy

**GitHub Enhance** does not collect, transmit, or sell any user data.

## What it does

- Runs only on `github.com`.
- Reads the current GitHub page in your browser to change how it looks (for
  example, reformatting dates, hiding sidebar sections, and applying the pull
  request redesign). This happens entirely on your device; page content is
  never sent anywhere.
- Saves your settings (which features are on and their options) with Chrome's
  storage API so they persist and, if you are signed in to Chrome, sync across
  your devices through your own Google account. These settings contain only
  your preferences, no personal information, and the developer cannot access
  them.

## What it does not do

- No analytics, tracking, or telemetry.
- No external servers or network requests.
- No remote code.
- No selling, sharing, or transferring of data to anyone.
